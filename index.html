About

My research journey is driven by a fundamental question: how can we bridge the gap between biological intelligence and artificial systems? The brain, with its remarkable efficiency and adaptability, offers a blueprint for the next generation of computing architectures.

I am particularly fascinated by the computational principles underlying neural information processing. Through my work in spiking neuron simulations and biophysical modeling, I aim to understand not just what neurons do, but how they do it — and how these mechanisms can be translated into efficient neuromorphic and brain-inspired computational systems, with relevance to large-scale neural modeling, adaptive intelligence, and brain-computer interfaces.

My interdisciplinary background in mathematics and ongoing studies in AI equip me to approach these challenges from both theoretical and applied perspectives.

Research Vision

My research interests focus on computational neuroscience and neuromorphic computing, with an emphasis on brain-inspired models for efficient intelligence. I am interested in understanding how biological principles such as spike-based communication, synaptic plasticity, and neural dynamics can be translated into spiking neural networks and low-power learning systems. My long-term goal is to pursue doctoral research that integrates theoretical modeling with practical neuromorphic and AI applications.

SmartShield: On-Device SMS Fraud Detection Using Spiking Neural Networks
Completed
Neuromorphic AI · Privacy-Preserving ML · Cybersecurity

A privacy-preserving, fully on-device SMS fraud detection system targeting smishing, OTP fraud, and evolving scam patterns in the Indian mobile context. Combines rule-based pre-filtering with a 1D Convolutional SNN classifier using Leaky Integrate-and-Fire neurons for zero-cloud, energy-efficient inference. Built at NIT Hamirpur Hackathon (Team of 2).

  • Latency-based spike encoding via ti = Tmax(1 − xi), converting feature vectors into sparse spike trains
  • Dataset: UCI SMS Spam Collection augmented with Indian-context samples (UPI fraud, KYC scams, OTP theft); features include TF-IDF, URL detection, urgency scoring, and sender anomaly flags
  • SNN model achieves ~444× fewer MACs and ~14,000× lower estimated energy consumption vs. ANN baseline
  • Deployment prototype on Android (Kotlin) with PyTorch .pt SNN model; ONNX optimization in progress
Tools: PyTorch + snnTorch Brian2 ONNX Runtime Mobile Android / Kotlin Edge AI
